Tips ▼
92T0→UPT→11X (PILOT)
Train at Columbus, Laughlin, or Vance AFB. All three use the T-6 Texan II for primary training. The advanced track is T-38C Talon (jets), being replaced by T-7A Red Hawk at select bases. Receive an 11-series AFSC based on assigned airframe after graduation.
92T0E→ENJJPT→11X (PILOT)
Euro-NATO Joint Jet Pilot Training at Sheppard AFB. All-jet pipeline (T-6 then T-38). No T-1 track. Graduates almost exclusively receive fighter or bomber assignments.
92T0H→Helicopter UPT→11H (RESCUE PILOT)
Helicopter pilot training at Ft. Rucker. Graduates typically fly HH-60 Pave Hawk or UH-1N Huey.
92T1→CSO Training→12X (CSO)
Combat Systems Officer training at Pensacola NAS. Outcomes: 12B (B-52 WSO), 12F (F-15E WSO), 12M (KC-135), 12R (E-3 Recon/EW), 12S (MC-130J Special Ops).
92T2→ABM Training→13B (AIR BATTLE MANAGER)
Air Battle Manager training at Tyndall AFB. ABMs direct air battles from E-3 AWACS, E-8 JSTARS, or ground BMC2 platforms.
92T3→RPA Training→18X (RPA PILOT)
RPA training at JBSA Randolph. Outcomes: 18A (MQ-9 Reaper), 18R (RQ-4 Global Hawk), 18S (Special Ops RPA).
Training Aircraft
T-6 Texan II — primary trainer at Columbus, Laughlin, Vance, and Sheppard.
T-38C Talon — advanced jet track at Columbus, Laughlin, Vance, and Sheppard (ENJJPT).
Note: the T-1 Jayhawk has been retired and is no longer flown.
T-7A Red Hawk — replacing T-38C; currently fielding at select bases.
